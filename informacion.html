For our guests

Information

Everything you need to plan the trip. We can't wait to welcome you to Mexico.

When

Saturday, April 10, 2027 - Ceremony and celebration.

Monday (April 12) or Tuesday (April 13) - Trip to the town of Tequila. Book your stay accordingly if you want to join us!

Where

Quinta San Carlos · San Pedro Tesistán, Jocotepec.

On the shores of Lake Chapala, Jalisco - about one hour from Guadalajara airport.

Flights

Fly into Guadalajara International Airport (GDL). It's the closest hub and has direct flights from most major cities in the US, Europe, and Latin America.

We recommend arriving on Thursday (April 8) or Friday (April 9) and leaving on Monday (April 12) if you're in a rush, or start a Mexican roadtrip after Monday (April 12)

Getting there

Communicate to us when you will be landing and we will be giving you some recommendation

We are probably going to organise buses from Guadalajara. We keep you posted as the situation will come closer.

By car to Quinta San Carlos: about 1 hour from GDL airport (~60 km), about 1h15 from Guadalajara center (~65 km). Uber and private taxis are also available.

Where to stay

Arriving before Thursday (April 8)? Enjoy Guadalajara first, then move to Lake Chapala (Ajijic, Jocotepec, Chapala) on Friday (April 9) - about 1h15 by car from the city center.

Best option: book directly at Quinta San Carlos from Thursday (April 8) to Sunday (April 11) - 150+ rooms right at the venue, so you're on site for the full experience.

Would you like to stay at the wedding venue? Send us a WhatsApp message and we'll help with your request - scan a code or tap a name.

Monse Mikel

Arriving Thursday (April 8) or Friday (April 9)? Head straight from the airport to your accommodation near the venue.

Passport & visa

A valid passport is required. Most EU, US, and many other nationalities don't need a visa for short stays - please verify based on your country.

Make sure your passport is valid for at least 6 months after the trip.

Weather & dress

April in Jalisco is warm and dry: ~26 °C / 79 °F days, cool evenings around 14 °C / 57 °F.

Bring a light layer for the evening. Dress code: garden formal - heels are not ideal for the lawn.

Your safety

We've planned the weekend with safe transport, trusted accommodations, and on-site support. If anything comes up, we're a message away.

After the wedding

As we will have a lot of international guests, we will also announce a post-wedding program for Sunday (April 11), Monday (April 12) and Tuesday (April 13), and you are invited to join us - for instance a trip to Tequila. Let us know your interest and we will share further information.

Up for Tequila? Join our WhatsApp group in your language and post it there - that's where we'll plan the trip together.
