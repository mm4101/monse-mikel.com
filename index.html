The Venue

Quinta San Carlos

Lake Chapala · Jalisco · Mexico

A boutique estate on the shores of Lake Chapala - Mexico's largest lake - wrapped in gardens, mountains, and the warm light of Jalisco.

We chose Quinta San Carlos for its open gardens and quiet beauty. A place to bring our worlds together and share a weekend of Mexican warmth, music, and good company.

Getting there

From Guadalajara to the celebration

About 50 minutes by car - 55 km from Guadalajara to Quinta San Carlos, on the western shore of Lake Chapala. From Guadalajara Airport (GDL): about 25 minutes to the city centre and 45 minutes to the venue.
