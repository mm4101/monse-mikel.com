Getting there · by plane

The closest airport is EuroAirport Basel–Mulhouse–Freiburg (BSL / MLH), about 40 min by car from Chalampé.

Also within reach: Strasbourg (SXB) ~1h20 and Zürich (ZRH) ~1h15 by car.

Getting there · by train

Closest stations to Chalampé, in order of preference:

1 · Müllheim (Baden), Germany - the nearest station, a short drive away. Neuenburg am Rhein is even closer, but usually needs one extra change.

2 · Mulhouse, France - a bigger hub with TGV to Paris, Strasbourg and Basel, about 15 min by car.

3 · Basel, Switzerland - well connected internationally, about 40 min by car.

From any of them it's a quick hop - taxi, or let us know and we'll help with a lift.

Getting there · by car

Chalampé sits right on the Rhine - the German town of Neuenburg am Rhein is just across the bridge.

Roughly 15 min from Mulhouse and 40 min from Basel / the EuroAirport.

Our top tip: rent a car

However you arrive - plane, train or car - our honest advice is to rent a car. It's by far the easiest way to reach Chalampé, and it lets you explore Alsace, the Black Forest and the Swiss border over a few days. Well worth it.

Where to stay

Handy bases nearby: Mulhouse (~15 min, most hotels), Neuenburg am Rhein just over the German border, and the villages around Ottmarsheim and Bantzenheim closer by.

Tell us if you'd like a tip - happy to point you to a good spot.
