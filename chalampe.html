Getting there · by plane

The closest airport is EuroAirport Basel–Mulhouse–Freiburg (BSL / MLH), about 40 min by car from Chalampé.

Also within reach: Strasbourg (SXB) ~1h20 and Zürich (ZRH) ~1h15 by car.

Getting there · by train

The main hub is Mulhouse, with TGV connections to Paris, Strasbourg and Basel.

From Mulhouse it's about 15 min by car to Chalampé (taxi or a lift from us).

Getting there · by car

Chalampé sits right on the Rhine - the German town of Neuenburg am Rhein is just across the bridge.

Roughly 15 min from Mulhouse and 40 min from Basel / the EuroAirport.

Where to stay

Handy bases nearby: Mulhouse (~15 min, most hotels), Neuenburg am Rhein just over the German border, and the villages around Ottmarsheim and Bantzenheim closer by.

Tell us if you'd like a tip - happy to point you to a good spot.

What to expect

Relaxed and informal · no DJ, no classic wedding traditions. Just good drinks, food, a bit of music, and time together. Come as you are.

An intimate gathering, with part of Monse's family travelling all the way from Mexico to be here.

This is our civil wedding in France - the big celebration follows in Mexico in April 2027.
